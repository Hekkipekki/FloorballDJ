License questions

Good to know.

Can I use the app without internet?

The trial is checked online at startup. An activated full license receives a signed offline certificate that normally remains valid for 30 days before the next check.

Can reinstalling start a new trial?

No. The trial is tied to the computer on the server and continues from its original start date.

Are my audio files changed or compressed?

No. Analysis and playback settings are stored in the profile. Original files are never rewritten.
