Features & interface

Fast when it matters.
Precise when you prepare.

FloorballDJ combines an immediate soundboard with a modern music workflow without hiding the controls that matter during a game.

Everything in its place

Built for quick decisions,
not buried menus.

When the game is running, the right sound should be one click away. Prepare the details calmly, then keep your attention on the action.

▦
01

Dynamic decks

Organise music and jingles in custom decks. Set rows and columns per deck, reorder them and colour-code buttons for instant recognition.

⌁
02

Smooth transitions

Global or individual fade times create controlled changes without sudden jumps in level.

≋
03

Autoplay and queue

Build playlists, reorder songs by drag and drop and choose looping, shuffle or controlled playback.

◉
04

Preview output

Send the next sound to headphones through a separate output before the audience hears it.

⌁
05

Consistent loudness

Analyse LUFS and true peak. FloorballDJ balances playback without compressing or rewriting the original files.

⤨
06

Build your own jingle

Combine two or more sounds, trim every part and choose precisely where the next sound begins. Source files remain untouched.
