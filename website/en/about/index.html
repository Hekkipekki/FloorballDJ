About FloorballDJ

Created close to the sport.
Tested in the real world.

A modern working tool for the people responsible for music and sound during floorball games.

The background

Match day drives development.

FloorballDJ is developed for people who need to find and play the right sound immediately while the game continues in front of them. The goal is the directness of a traditional jingle player, combined with a modern interface and better control of today's audio libraries.

The beta improves together with users in real match environments. Stability, clarity and fast workflows therefore take priority over unnecessary features.

Common questions

The app in brief.

Which audio formats are supported?

FloorballDJ is built for common music and jingle formats such as MP3, WAV and M4A. Exact compatibility will be verified before public launch.

Is FloorballDJ only for floorball?

The interface follows the flow of a floorball game, but the same decks, queues and jingles can be used at other sporting events.

How can I join the beta?

The first beta is shared with selected testers. Public download opens once installation, licensing and the critical match workflows have been verified.
